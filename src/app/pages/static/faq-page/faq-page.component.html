Frequently Asked Questions

What is Work iFence?

Work iFence is an online platform that offers services to manage resumes and job applications. It also provides the latest job openings, learning resources, and training materials.

What kinds of training resources does Work iFence offer?

Work iFence provides a list of online and in-person 1:1 or group training sessions from experts across the globe. It also offers highly rated training videos from sources such as YouTube, Udemy, and more.

Where does Work iFence Store Created Resumes?

Work iFence stores generated resumes in S3 storage in PDF format and stores the actual data in JSON format in the Work iFence database as a TextBlob.

What is Work iFence AI Support Feature?

Work iFence is integrated with ChatGPT open API, and provides you on-demand support for the selected sections of Resume based on your workIfence Subscription level.

Can I post my own deals or deals I find on Work iFence?

Unfortunately, this is not a feature we provide. Although we hope to incorporate this into our site soon enough!

Can I save deals for later on Work iFence?

The wishlist feature is one that is under the works right now and will be released in our very next launch.
